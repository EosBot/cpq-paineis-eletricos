# Context

## Ubiquitous Language

### Tenant
A `Tenant` is a panel manufacturer using the platform as an isolated business space. Catalog, rules, customers, quotes, BOMs, orders, and memberships are always scoped to a single tenant.

### User
A `User` is a person authenticated by the platform. A user may belong to one or more tenants.

### Membership
A `Membership` links a user to a tenant and defines the role and permissions that apply only inside that tenant.

### Role
A `Role` is a tenant-scoped permission profile derived from a platform base matrix and customized by the tenant.

### CustomerAccount
A `CustomerAccount` is a B2B customer company of a tenant. Quotes and commercial relationships are attached to the customer account.

### CustomerContact
A `CustomerContact` is a person associated with a customer account. A quote may reference one primary contact.

### CommercialContract
A `CommercialContract` is an optional commercial agreement between a tenant and a customer account. When active, it defines price conditions used during quote calculation. When absent, the tenant default pricing applies.

### CustomerCatalogRestriction
A `CustomerCatalogRestriction` is a commercial restriction derived from a commercial contract that limits which tenant components may be quoted for a specific customer account.

### ComponentCategory
A `ComponentCategory` defines the template of attributes that components of the same kind must satisfy.

### Component
A `Component` is a catalog item owned by a tenant and used in configurations, BOMs, and pricing.

### ComponentAttribute
A `ComponentAttribute` stores a typed attribute value of a component according to the template defined by its component category.

### Configuration
A `Configuration` is the editable technical definition of a panel being assembled for a quote while that quote is in draft.

### ConfigurationSnapshot
A `ConfigurationSnapshot` is the immutable frozen state of a configuration captured when a quote leaves draft and enters internal review.

### Rule
A `Rule` is a declarative tenant-owned business rule used for validation, compatibility, sizing, electrical logic, pricing, or tax composition.

### RuleVersion
A `RuleVersion` is an immutable approved version of a rule. Quotes always reference the rule versions used in calculation.

### Quote
A `Quote` is the commercial aggregate that links a tenant, a customer account, a configuration, calculated prices, lifecycle status, and downstream order conversion.

### QuoteVersion
A `QuoteVersion` is an immutable commercial snapshot generated by recalculation or reopening, preserving the exact configuration snapshot, rule versions, and pricing inputs used.

### BOM
A `BOM` is the material structure derived from a configuration and its rules.

### BOMVersion
A `BOMVersion` is a versioned immutable material snapshot tied to a quote version and configuration snapshot.

### Order
A `Order` is the commercial order created from an approved quote and becomes the root of the operational post-approval flow.

### Shipment
A `Shipment` is the delivery aggregate associated with an order, preserving delivery data and the future shipping integration boundary.

### ShippingQuote
A `ShippingQuote` is a freight pricing option associated with a shipment. The model exists before provider selection so the domain can evolve toward future integration.

## Lifecycle Language

### Quote Status
The canonical quote lifecycle is: `Draft` → `InternalReview` → `Calculated` → `Sent` → `CustomerApproved` or `Rejected` or `Expired`.

### Order Status
The canonical order lifecycle is: `Confirmed` → `InProduction` → `ReadyToShip` → `Shipped` → `Delivered`.

## Domain Rules

- The platform starts with row-level tenant isolation using `tenantId`.
- The model must preserve a future path to stronger isolation such as schema-per-tenant or database-per-tenant.
- `Configuration` is mutable only while the quote is in `Draft`.
- Entering `InternalReview` creates the immutable snapshot used by `QuoteVersion` and `BOMVersion`.
- `Engineer` edits configuration and submits it to internal review.
- `Sales` calculates and sends quotes.
- `Admin` approves exceptions and may reopen expired quotes.
- `InternalReview` is the internal approval stage.
- `CustomerApproved` is the external customer acceptance of the quote.
- Reopening an expired quote creates a new quote version and triggers recalculation against the current configuration or a chosen snapshot.
- Currency is single per tenant in the initial scope.
- Taxes are declarative pricing components resolved from the commercial contract or tenant default pricing.
- `Order` must preserve the domain boundary for future freight integration through `Shipment` and `ShippingQuote`.
- A `CommercialContract` may restrict which tenant components are commercially available to a customer account.
- A `CommercialContract` does not create new components and does not override tenant technical rules.
